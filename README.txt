📊 Amazon Prime Content Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

This project analyzes the Amazon Prime Video dataset to explore the distribution of movies and TV shows, trends over time, and key patterns in ratings, genres, and geography.

🔍 Project Overview

The dataset contains details about shows on Amazon Prime, including:

Title, Type (Movie/TV Show)

Director & Cast

Country

Release Year & Date Added

Rating

Duration

Genre & Description

The goal of this project was to turn raw data into an interactive, insightful dashboard that answers:

How is content distributed across countries?

What is the balance between Movies vs TV Shows?

Which ratings and genres dominate the platform?

How has content grown over time?

⚙️ Steps I Followed

Imported Data – Loaded CSV dataset into Power BI.

Data Cleaning – Removed blanks, formatted dates, normalized categories.

Exploration – Applied conditional formatting, mapped fields, grouped categories.

Dashboard Design – Built multiple visuals:

🌍 Map → Titles by country

🍩 Donut chart → Movies vs TV Shows

📈 Area chart → Titles released over time

📊 Bar chart → Ratings distribution

🎭 Genre & duration insights

🔢 Cards → Total titles, total movies, total shows

Branding & Formatting – Used custom colors, labels, legends, and an Amazon Prime logo for professional polish.

💡 Insights Gained

The United States & India lead in total content produced.

Movies are more common than TV Shows, though TV Shows are steadily increasing.

The highest surge of content came between 2017–2019.

TV-MA and TV-14 ratings dominate, showing a focus on mature audiences.

Genres are highly diverse, with a strong representation of international films.

🛠 Tools & Skills Used

Power BI (Dashboarding, DAX, Visualization)

Data Cleaning & Transformation

Data Storytelling & Insights Communication
